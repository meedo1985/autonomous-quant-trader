# Owner decision — defer narrow DSR calibration

**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Decision:** **DEFER**
**Authority:** explicit owner instruction in the project conversation

The owner accepted the recommendation to defer the narrow conventional DSR
baseline experiment. Its successful calibration would not establish a practical
promotion method, while the broader numerical draft conflicts with the
candidate's supported domain and event definition.

Consequences:

- do not implement a DSR calibration engine;
- do not generate development or held-out simulation results;
- keep DSR diagnostic-only and the mandatory 0.95 gate unsatisfied;
- keep Task 13, governed trials, confirmation/lockbox access, and promotion
  blocked;
- preserve every proposal and failed/superseded design as review evidence.

This decision is not a governance amendment, scientific-method acceptance,
cycle activation, or permission to trade. Future reconsideration requires a
broader reviewed method, a consistent preregistration, independent numerical
references, human/statistician acceptance, and the formal amendment process.
