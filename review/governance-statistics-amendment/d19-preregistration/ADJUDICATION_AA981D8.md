**Result:** `PREREGISTRATION.md` revision 2

All 24 findings are accepted. Where the reviewers overlap, one disposition covers both. The drafter's own arithmetic in rev 1 §6 was wrong for the availability shares, as both reviewers showed: a true rate of 0.0012 passes a 0.003 share with probability 0.953, not above 0.99. Rev 2 replaces fixed margins with a frozen exact-binomial rule.
