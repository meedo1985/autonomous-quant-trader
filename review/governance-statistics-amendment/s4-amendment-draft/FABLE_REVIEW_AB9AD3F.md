## Plain-language summary for the owner

1. The new gate annex faithfully restates the gate rules you chose, and the O-1..O-7 answers went into the draft correctly; most fixes are small wording and bookkeeping corrections.
2. The public-seed plan has a hole: unless the posting channel is one fixed account where nothing can be deleted or backdated, someone could post several hashes and keep the luckiest seed. The channel rules must be written down before you pick one (O-6a).
3. One consequence was never shown to you: the new order and fill rules change every normal backtest and the benchmark itself, not only the stress tests. You should be asked to confirm that.
4. Even with the beacon, whoever declares can still abort a cycle after seeing the seed and start again. Each restart costs one halving of the false-promotion allowance, and the draft should say so openly.
5. Nothing is activated. After these fixes, the remaining owner items (REG-1, REG-2, O-6a, plus the new confirmation item) can go to you together.
