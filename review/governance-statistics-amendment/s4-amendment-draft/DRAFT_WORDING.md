## 3. Rationale, as §4 requires

Rewrite this in your own words when you author it. A short form:

> The v1.0 DSR clause names an effective-count method that was never defined
> and a 0.95 threshold with no stated error rate (D-16, D-19). This amendment
> fixes one selection rule (each family's best-Sharpe declared trial, one
> look, no replacement), lets only never-evaluated confirmation data promote,
> replaces the count-based penalty with a bootstrap of the family's own
> returns, and caps false promotion at 5% in the first eligible cycle,
> halving in each later one, so the lifetime total stays under 10%. A human
> statistician did not review it; two different AI model families did.
