### Must fix

1. **HK-R1 — HIGH — missed freeze read has no stop rule** ([sheet, J](/D:/PMP-programs-for-sharawi/aqt-freeze-sheet/deploy/calibration/FREEZE_HELDOUT.md:103)). If the laptop is offline at the scheduled time, a later CDX query cannot reproduce the index as it stood at the freeze point. A later read could admit a capture indexed in the meantime and change the selected round. **Repair:** state that a missed or unproved on-time read stops the attempt for the owner’s decision; never treat a delayed query as the freeze read. This is also the stop required by FP4N-R2.

2. **HK-R2 — HIGH — the drand-to-run bound can be reset without a rule** ([sheet, K](/D:/PMP-programs-for-sharawi/aqt-freeze-sheet/deploy/calibration/FREEZE_HELDOUT.md:127)). If record 1 misses its deadline of 24 hours after the drand commit, “make record 1 again” cannot satisfy that deadline. It permits an unbounded wait after the output is known, the exposure FP3S4-G2 sought to limit. **Repair:** keep the first drand commitment as the timing anchor and specify a stop for the owner’s decision when its deadline is missed; a fresh invalidation check alone must not restart that clock.

3. **HK-R3 — HIGH — the node may lose the required history before certification** ([sheet, M](/D:/PMP-programs-for-sharawi/aqt-freeze-sheet/deploy/calibration/FREEZE_HELDOUT.md:168); [laptop steps](/D:/PMP-programs-for-sharawi/aqt-freeze-sheet/review/fp4-node/FP4_LAPTOP_NODE_STEPS.txt:36)). The laptop uses `prune=25000`, a moving size target. Keeping block 972,269 through the freeze read does not ensure it remains available through a weeks-long held-out run. Stopping at “MARGIN LOW” preserves history but prevents a current-tip scan; freeing space on D: alone does not change the prune target. **Repair:** before H, establish and verify a storage and node-setting plan that retains the block range while remaining current through the M check, or specify another approved verifier path. Core documents that an automatic prune target deletes older block files as storage grows. [Bitcoin Core source](https://github.com/bitcoin/bitcoin/blob/v31.1/src/node/blockstorage.cpp).

4. **HK-R4 — HIGH — fee estimate is timed to signing, not broadcast** ([sheet, H.3](/D:/PMP-programs-for-sharawi/aqt-freeze-sheet/deploy/calibration/FREEZE_HELDOUT.md:80)). The preview, export, independent check and signing can leave a substantial gap before broadcast. An estimate taken up to an hour before signing does not establish the required fee at broadcast, and the instruction does not repeat the check for a replacement. **Repair:** record a fresh `estimatesmartfee 6` immediately before each broadcast and compare its numeric `feerate` with the transaction’s actual sat/vB. The BTC/kvB × 100,000 conversion is correct. [Core RPC documentation](https://bitcoincore.org/en/doc/31.0.0/rpc/util/estimatesmartfee/).

### Should fix

5. **HK-R5 — MEDIUM — laptop RPC instructions are not executable as written** ([sheet, H0](/D:/PMP-programs-for-sharawi/aqt-freeze-sheet/deploy/calibration/FREEZE_HELDOUT.md:61)). `getblock` takes a **hash**, not height 972,269; the operator first needs `getblockhash 972269`. `scanblocks` puts `filter_false_positives` in its sixth-argument options object, and `getdescriptoractivity` requires both the block-hash array and the descriptor array. The prose does not give Windows-safe invocations for those arguments. A literal attempt can fail at the post gate. **Repair:** add and smoke-test the full PowerShell/`bitcoin-cli` commands, including the required arguments and `-rpcclienttimeout=0` for long scans. The specified `completed: true`, heights and index checks are sound. [getblock](https://bitcoincore.org/en/doc/31.0.0/rpc/blockchain/getblock/), [scanblocks](https://bitcoincore.org/en/doc/31.0.0/rpc/blockchain/scanblocks/), [getdescriptoractivity](https://bitcoincore.org/en/doc/31.0.0/rpc/blockchain/getdescriptoractivity/).

6. **HK-R6 — MEDIUM — drand fetch route and source wording are ambiguous** ([sheet, K](/D:/PMP-programs-for-sharawi/aqt-freeze-sheet/deploy/calibration/FREEZE_HELDOUT.md:118)). Hostnames alone do not identify quicknet; `/public/<round>` serves drand’s default chain. The verifier rejects a wrong-chain signature, so this is an availability and runbook failure, not a verifier bypass. The later phrase “never permits another … source” also conflicts with FP3 Rule 4, which permits any endpoint carrying the bound round. **Repair:** give chain-specific full URI templates for both relays and say “another chain, round, or randomness source,” while allowing another relay for the same bound round. [drand v1 chain route](https://docs.drand.love/developer/API-v1/chain-hash-public-round/), [drand v2 example and relay support](https://docs.drand.love/developer/http-api/).
