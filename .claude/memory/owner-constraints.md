The owner of `autonomous-quant-trader` stated on 2026-09-21 that he is not a
statistician and cannot become one quickly, that he cannot send the work to
anyone outside ("i need it to me only"), and that the system **will trade his own
real capital** — his money, nobody else's.

**Why:** these three together decide what advice is useful. The project's
governance asks for a qualified human statistician who will never arrive, so
answers that depend on obtaining one are dead ends. And because he cannot verify
statistical output himself, an overstated claim reaches him unchallenged.

**How to apply:** do not keep proposing external review as the path. Do the
statistics directly and explain them — that is legitimate and useful, and it is
distinct from an AI signing a reviewer decision form as a qualified human, which
is not. Prefer the reading that raises a safety hurdle when an input is
uncertain, and say so. State uncertainty plainly rather than rounding it away:
he is relying on the output exactly where he cannot check it. See
[[quant-trader-project-state]] and [[ai-review-record-discipline]].
